Research Projects

Our research spans the intersection of digital transformation and cyber-physical systems across multiple application domains.

Active & Completed Research

Ongoing
Digital Twin Ecosystem for Computer Numberical Control Manufacturing Processes
Development of a generic, real-time Digital Twin Ecosystem (DTE) for both additive and subtractive manufacturing processes. Integrates IoT sensors, edge computing, and game-engine visualization (Unity 3D) to create synchronized physical-digital representations enabling real-time monitoring, anomaly detection, and predictive control of manufacturing operations. Current research is extending the ecosystem toward a product-side Digital Twin, exploring implicit modeling and constructive solid geometry to represent the manufactured part itself.
Digital Manufacturing
Ongoing
Blockchain-Enabled Distributed Manufacturing
Design and implementation of a decentralized production system that lets geographically distributed facilities operate as one additive manufacturing (FDM) unit, with a Hub and Remote Cell coordinating part design, printing, and inspection. Recent work integrates blockchain and a Fused Filament Fabrication asset Digital Twin: smart contracts automate cross-facility workflows and enforce secure, traceable, tamper-resistant data exchange, while the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) stores large CAD models and real-time monitoring data off-chain to keep the ledger scalable. Backed by a STRIDE threat-model analysis and designed for small and medium-sized manufacturers, the system removes the need for centralized control while simplifying and standardizing distributed manufacturing.

Completed
CNN-Based Sunlit-Leaf Segmentation for Precision Irrigation
A deep-learning approach to precision irrigation that uses convolutional neural networks to segment sunlit leaves, the foliage that reveals water stress earliest, from visible-spectrum canopy images for a more accurate Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI). The project released the first labeled sunlit-leaf segmentation dataset for pistachio trees and benchmarked four CNN architectures (FRRN-A, FC-DenseNet103, ResNet101-DeepLabV3, and SegNet), improving segmentation accuracy and precision by over 100% and 350% respectively versus the prior state of the art, and was later extended to tomato plants in follow-up work presented at the AAAI Workshop on AI for Agriculture and Food Systems. The resulting expert system is offered as a free web-based tool, developed with the Center of Irrigation Technology (CIT) at California State University, Fresno, and funded by the California Department of Food & Agriculture (CDFA).

Ongoing
IoT-Enabled Condition Monitoring for Overall Equipment Effectiveness
A real-time temperature and humidity monitoring system that applies the Internet of Things (IoT) to condition monitoring for Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) on the Industry 4.0 factory floor, addressing the need for cost-effective solutions that support digital transformation in smart factories. Sensor data is acquired, distributed, and dashboarded in real time on an IoT edge-computing platform, which notifies stakeholders of deviations by e-mail and surfaces the dashboards in an Extended Reality (XR) smart-factory setting. The system's monitoring capabilities were validated through two pilot experiments, with ongoing work extending the platform toward predictive maintenance.
