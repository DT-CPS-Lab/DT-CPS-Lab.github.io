Open PhD Position

A fully funded PhD position in the Digital Transformation & Cyber-Physical Systems Laboratory at the University of Central Florida — tuition fully covered and a competitive stipend.

The Opportunity

The DT&CPS Lab is seeking a motivated PhD student to join our team in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Systems (IEMS) at UCF. This is a fully funded doctoral position supporting research at the intersection of Digital Twins, real-time simulation, and advanced manufacturing. You will work closely with the PI and an interdisciplinary team, contribute to high-impact publications, and help build systems that connect the physical and digital worlds.

Tuition Fully Covered

Full tuition coverage for the duration of the funded PhD assistantship.

Competitive Stipend

A competitive annual stipend to support your studies and research.

Flexible Start

Position open for the next available term; applications reviewed on a rolling basis.

What You'll Work On

Our research bridges physical systems and their digital counterparts. The exact focus is shaped to your strengths and interests, drawing on themes such as:

Digital Twins

High-fidelity, continuously synchronized virtual replicas of physical assets and processes.

Real-Time Modeling & Simulation

Interactive, high-performance simulation of dynamic systems and processes.

Advanced & Digital Manufacturing

Smart manufacturing processes, digital manufacturing workflows, and Industry 4.0 integration.

Computer Graphics & Interactive 3D

Geometric modeling, 3D visualization, and GPU-accelerated interactive environments.

Internet of Things

Sensor-driven, connected cyber-physical systems from edge devices to the cloud.

AI-Driven Systems

Machine learning and data-driven methods that make cyber-physical systems intelligent.

Who We're Looking For

We welcome candidates with a background in computer science, computer engineering, or a related field. Curiosity, strong programming skills, and a drive to build real systems matter more to us than any rigid checklist.

  • Solid programming skills and a genuine interest in building software.
  • Experience or interest in one or more of: computer graphics, GPU programming, real-time simulation, or digital twins.
  • Curiosity about cybersecurity, the Internet of Things, and AI-driven systems is a plus.
  • Self-motivated, collaborative, and excited to work across disciplines.

How to Apply

Ready to Apply?

Email Dr. Minas Pantelidakis with your CV, transcripts, and a short statement of your research interests. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until the position is filled — we encourage you to reach out early.
