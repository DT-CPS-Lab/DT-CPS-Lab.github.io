Research

Our Research

Pushing the boundaries of Digital Twin technology through rigorous academic research with direct industry applications in smart manufacturing and intelligent systems.

Focus Areas

Primary Research Areas

Digital Twins for CNC Machining

Development of high-fidelity Digital Twin models for CNC machining and manufacturing processes. These systems enable real-time synchronization between physical machines and their virtual counterparts, supporting predictive maintenance, process optimization, and quality assurance.

CNC Machining Process Monitoring Predictive Maintenance Quality Control

GPU-Accelerated Simulation Frameworks

Design and implementation of massively parallel simulation architectures on modern GPU hardware. By leveraging CUDA and compute shaders, we achieve real-time manufacturing process simulation that was previously computationally infeasible, enabling true Digital Twin deployments.

CUDA Real-Time Sim Parallel Computing HPC

Signed Distance Fields & Material Removal

Novel SDF-based geometric representations for efficient, accurate simulation of material removal in subtractive manufacturing. Our methods provide continuous differentiable geometry representations ideal for real-time collision detection and machining simulation at unprecedented speed and fidelity.

SDF Geometry Processing Material Removal Collision Detection

IoT Integration & Smart Manufacturing

Seamless integration of IoT sensor networks, edge computing infrastructure, and cloud platforms with Digital Twin systems. We develop closed-loop architectures where real-time sensor data continuously updates the Digital Twin, enabling adaptive manufacturing and intelligent process control.

IoT Sensors Edge Computing Cloud Integration Closed-Loop Control

Computer Vision in Industrial Settings

Application of advanced computer vision techniques — including deep learning-based object detection, pose estimation, and semantic segmentation — to industrial quality inspection, assembly verification, and workplace safety monitoring in manufacturing environments.

Deep Learning Quality Inspection Object Detection 3D Vision

Machine Learning for Predictive Manufacturing

Data-driven ML and AI frameworks for predictive quality control, tool wear estimation, surface finish prediction, and process parameter optimization. We combine physics-informed models with data-driven approaches for robust, generalizable manufacturing intelligence.

Neural Networks Predictive QC Physics-Informed ML Tool Wear

Roadmap

Research Timeline

Our development trajectory from foundational research to industry-scale deployment.

2022 — Foundation
GPU-SDF Framework v1
Initial GPU-accelerated SDF computation for single-operation CNC simulation achieving 60× speedup over CPU baselines.
2023 — Expansion
IoT Integration & ML Models
Real-time IoT data ingestion pipeline and first ML models for tool wear prediction achieving 94% accuracy.
2024 — KDT Launch
Industry Deployment via KDT
Knights Digital Twin initiative launched; first industry partner Digital Twins deployed in production manufacturing environments.
2025–2026 — Scale
Multi-Machine & Full-Plant DT
Scaling to full manufacturing cell and plant-level Digital Twins with autonomous optimization and zero-defect targets.
